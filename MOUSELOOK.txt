Optional horizontal mouse-look

During 3D exploration, press C or click the mouse wheel to switch between the
original arrow controls and mouse-look. The same saved option is on Options ->
Settings -> page 4: 3D mouse (German: Maussteuerung 3D, Pfeile/Mausblick).

Mouse-look turns left/right only. For first-person keyboard controls, choose
QWEASD under 3D Movement: W/S move, A/D strafe and Q/E turn. Space still
interacts with what is directly in front of you.

The viewport, frame and full interface remain unchanged and visible. Press C
or click the wheel again to release the pointer for clicking the controls.
Opening an inventory, dialog or options also releases the pointer;
returning to 3D exploration resumes the selected mode. 2D maps, switching away
from the window, and window/fullscreen transitions release mouse capture.

Arrows remain the default. The selected mode is remembered between sessions.
MouseLookSensitivity in ambermoon.cfg adjusts turning speed (25-300 percent,
default 100), independently of the keyboard movement speed.

This addition changes neither game balance nor the existing menu shortcuts.
