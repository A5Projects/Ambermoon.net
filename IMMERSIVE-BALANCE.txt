Immersive UI - relaxed balance

Based on the installed Original 1.22 / Advanced 1.33 data, running in the
Ambermoon.net 1.15.1 immersive branch. No versions.dat or save files are patched.

Advanced party progression
- Original base HP, SP, spell-learning points (SLP), and training points (TP)
  are used for future level-ups of the ten original playable companions.
  Existing randomized 50-100% gains and INT/25 magic bonuses still apply.
- Original bases, in HP / SP / SLP / TP order:
  Hero 10/8/5/6; Tar 6/16/10/10; Egil 16/0/0/8; Selena 8/0/0/14;
  Nelvin 6/16/10/10; Sabine 6/16/10/10; Valdyn 12/6/3/12;
  Targor 6/16/10/10; Leonaria 6/16/10/10; Gryban 14/6/3/10.
- Level-up tooltips use these same values. The Advanced stamina HP bonus is
  disabled. Existing HP, SP, points, attributes, skills, and caps are retained.
- XP thresholds and attack-per-round progression remain Advanced. In particular,
  existing levels are not recalculated. Advanced-only characters keep their data.
- Stored growth fields are retained: only the calculation of future gains uses
  the original table. XP-exchange services and their anti-farming guard remain.

Spells and other Advanced features
- Original spells use original learning prices and party casting prices.
- Advanced-only spells retain their learning and casting prices. Enemy casting
  prices and elemental altar upgrade pricing rules remain unchanged.
- Original destruction-spell base damage ranges are restored. Advanced elemental
  affinities, level/intelligence scaling, status effects, and physical damage
  calculations remain in place.
- New quests, locations, items, sages, transports, and other world interactions
  remain. Individual enemy stats and initial party skill/attribute data remain.

Convenience changes (both editions in this immersive build)
- Carrying capacity is four kilograms per effective strength point, instead of
  one. The original 999-gram rounding allowance, inventory slots, stack limits,
  strength itself, and stat caps are unchanged.
- Gold weighs one gram per coin instead of five (20% of original weight).
  Stored weights are recalculated on load, including unrecruited companions.
  Advanced food and ammunition reductions remain unchanged.
- Party physical attacks have an 85% baseline skill-check success chance before
  status penalties. Higher trained/equipment skill still applies. Saved skills
  are not inflated; enemy accuracy is unchanged. Defense, immunity and parries
  can still prevent damage, and blindness/drugged penalties still apply.
- Ordinary weapon catalog entries 181-201 have zero break chance: knife, dagger,
  short/long sword, scimitar, two-handed/holy sword, staff, club, flail, mace,
  morning star, hammer, axe/battle axe, trident, whip, sling, short/long bow and
  crossbow. This also prevents breakage when parrying. Shared definitions cover
  both sides. Armor, shields, later special weapons and already-broken items
  are unchanged. No automatic repair is performed.

Compatibility
- No save format changes, level recalculation, skill reset, point refunds, or
  retroactive level-up rewards. The existing save files are not rewritten during
  installation. Future saves use the existing format and store newly earned gains.
- The Original edition retains its own progression and spell costs while getting
  the shared carrying, gold, accuracy, and ordinary weapon conveniences.
