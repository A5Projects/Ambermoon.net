Optional exploration cheats

Enable the existing cheat console through Options -> Settings -> Enable cheats.

  noencounters 1   Disable random combat encounters while moving.
  notired 1        Prevent tiredness and exhaustion from lack of sleep.

Use 0 instead of 1 to disable either cheat, or omit the number to toggle it.
Both commands appear in help; help noencounters and help notired explain them.

Both default off. They last for the current application session, including
save reloads. Re-enter them after restarting the application.

noencounters preserves scripted/story battles and monsters visible on the map.
notired removes existing exhaustion and restores its reduced attributes/skills
without healing HP/SP. Time, poison, disease and spell durations still advance;
ordinary resting remains available.

This expanded build retains its standard balance. The immersive build's growth,
spell prices, hit chances, break chances, gold weight and capacity changes have
not been copied. The separate carryweight cheat remains available as before;
see CARRYWEIGHT.txt.
