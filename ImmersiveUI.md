# Immersive UI

This is the default `main` branch of [A5Projects/Ambermoon.net](https://github.com/A5Projects/Ambermoon.net), based on Ambermoon.net 1.15.1. Exploration fills the 320x163 logical pixel area below the original party portrait bar in both 2D and 3D. Original dialogs, inventory, shops, and battle layouts remain available. See [Controls](Controls.md) for the overlay controls.

The 2D renderer shows 20x11 tile slots, clips the partial bottom row, and keeps a two-tile rendering margin for scrolling. Normal movement animates the camera and player between their tile positions using whole original pixels. Collision, event activation, game time, and save coordinates still use the original tile steps. Movement retains the original step distances and nominal intervals; elapsed-time scheduling keeps its pace independent of render and update frequency. Crossing into a neighboring section of the same world preserves the ongoing scroll and walking cadence, including at the world's wrapping edges. Small indoor maps are centered where they do not fill the viewport; scrolling is clamped without reading beyond their edges. Picking accounts for the moving camera and rejects empty padding. Teleports, entering or leaving locations, and opening an interface settle immediately at the logical position.

The exploration action overlay reuses the existing action grid at the bottom-right edge and opens without changing the viewport or camera. Escape closes an existing window first, then toggles the exploration overlay. Enter, Tab, and MENU also toggle it. Movement buttons are hidden during exploration; keyboard and mouse movement still work. An opaque backing inside the button edges prevents the world showing through transparent icon pixels. The original monster sight behavior is kept separate from the larger display area.

The location and acquired indicators share one translucent bar below the portraits, with a thin black separator behind the portrait-name shadows. The location is left aligned and shortened names have a full-name tooltip. A compact black-metal compass stays centered on the screen; localized direction labels and intermediate ticks scroll under a fixed pointer. It is hidden until the compass is activated in the game. The clock and other indicators occupy the right side; coordinates use a second row only when needed. Active spells remain near the bottom left. Existing special-item and spell unlock rules are retained.

Optional horizontal mouse-look uses relative desktop mouse deltas with an unbounded cursor. C, middle-click, and the clickable 3D mouse option on settings page 4 switch modes; capture is suspended for UI screens, 2D, and focus loss. Backends without a disabled cursor use recentering. The existing WASD and QWEASD keyboard modes are preserved, including Space interaction. Vertical camera tilt is intentionally excluded. Keyboard movement defaults to 60% walking speed. Caps Lock toggles 100% sprint; holding either Shift key temporarily sprints and releasing it restores the selected mode. Caps Lock remembers its in-game selection independently of the keyboard's lock light; Shift never changes that selection. F11 cycles window modes.

3D walking and strafing use a smoothed display camera. The camera used for movement, collision, interactions, and saves keeps its original positions. Mouse-look and keyboard turning stay immediate. Movement runs against elapsed time at the nominal 60-tick rate; the game clocks retain fractional ticks instead of rounding each update. Catch-up after a stall is limited to 100 milliseconds. The original geometry, textures, projection, and frame settings are retained. Set `Smooth3DMovement` to `false` in `ambermoon.cfg` while the game is closed to compare with the previous movement display. This local trial is not included in the published v1.0.0 release.

The startup selection also determines balance. **Original** keeps original carrying capacity, gold weight, attack checks (including misses), weapon break chances, progression, and spell rules. **Advanced** includes our existing [relaxed balance](IMMERSIVE-BALANCE.txt): original party growth and spell costs where applicable, increased carrying capacity, lighter gold, improved party hit chances, and zero break chance for ordinary starter weapons. Existing progress and Advanced XP thresholds remain. Both selections retain the immersive visuals and controls. Optional `noencounters`, `notired`, and `carryweight` session cheats are available and start disabled. See [save transfer and carry capacity](CARRYWEIGHT.txt).

The full exploration viewport is always enabled in this branch. `Expanded3DView` remains in configuration for compatibility with the [`expanded` branch](https://github.com/A5Projects/Ambermoon.net/tree/expanded), which retains the framed interface and standard balance. Both branches offer Original and Advanced game data through the startup selector.

The existing action icons and right-click interaction behavior remain. A new context menu, redesigned dialogs, adaptive portrait sizes, and widescreen logical resolution are not part of this version. The game still scales its 320x200 logical canvas to the window.
